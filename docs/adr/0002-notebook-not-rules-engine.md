# Notebook, not rules engine

The app records what the judge says happened and never validates role restrictions, targeting, cooldowns, or win conditions. Players bring custom rules; enforcement would make those impossible and turn the notebook into a game engine that is wrong for every custom board. Ability prompts and peek reveals are guidance only.

## Consequences

- Every state change is judge-initiated.
- "Used" flags and table states are manual.
- No win detection or automatic elimination chains.
