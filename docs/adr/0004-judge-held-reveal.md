# Judge-held reveals

The judge keeps the device for every player-facing reveal: dealt roles and peek results are shown on a judge-held screen, then hidden. Players confirm by looking, not by tapping or taking the device. This is faster and removes the wrong-seat handover failure that a pass-and-play flow cannot detect, at the cost of relying on the judge to show the screen only to the named seat.

## Considered Options

- Pass-and-reveal handover. Rejected: an interstitial per seat, more handling, and no real protection against the wrong player taking the device.

## Consequences

- Reveal screens must be full-screen and hide on one judge tap.
- Reveal progress is draft state, not an event.
- Privacy depends on the judge physically presenting the screen; the app does not verify identity.
